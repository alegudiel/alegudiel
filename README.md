### 🔎 About me

I'm a Computer Science engineer (Universidad del Valle de Guatemala, 2024) currently working as a **Data Scientist at Zigi App**, building credit-risk pipelines and benchmarking LLMs for conversational AI trust and usability.

I'm moving into **HCI and Interaction Design**, starting an MSc in Human-Computer Interaction at the **Universität Siegen** this fall. My focus is conversational AI that works as a trustworthy companion for underserved users: people with limited digital access, older adults, anyone tech tends to design around instead of for.

---

### 🛠️ Featured projects

**[🐟 ACNH Critterpedia Tracker](https://github.com/alegudiel/CritterpediaTracker)** — Offline SwiftUI iOS app for tracking Animal Crossing: New Horizons critters. Built and tested with a real user (my sister), documented with a full UX case study from problem framing to design decisions.

**[🧘 StretchBreak](https://github.com/alegudiel/StretchBreak)** — Cross-platform (macOS/Windows/Linux) break reminder built with Electron + React. Ships with an embedded UX case study and an ongoing diary study.

**[🏨 Reservation System](https://github.com/alegudiel/ReservationSystem)** — Full-stack hotel reservation platform (React + Node/Express + MySQL) inspired by IRTRA hostels in Guatemala.
